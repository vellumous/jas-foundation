Our Impact

Five communities on the Cape Flats. Nine clinics. One growing network.

JAS Foundation is actively engaged in several communities across the Cape Flats — Athlone, Elsies River, Guguletu, Kenilworth and Manenberg — through a teaching clinic and the community clinics our trainees set up in their own neighbourhoods.

Athlone — the Teaching Clinic

The Teaching Clinic runs on the last Saturday of each month — earlier in December — and is open to everyone. Clients come from the surrounding neighbourhoods: Kewtown, Bridgetown, Manenberg, Silvertown, and Mitchells Plain.

It works like a teaching hospital: our trainees and practitioners treat clients under supervision while they learn. During 2025, they completed 274 sessions.
