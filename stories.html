Client Stories

The people we treat, in their own words.

We don't tell these stories for them. Where a client has agreed to be filmed, their interview sits below their words — link out to YouTube, no heavy video on the page.

"I had a very painful life until I started Bowen… (now) I can give my grandchild a better hug, I can open a tin of baked beans, I can bend to wash my hair — all things I couldn't do before."
Lynn Duffy — Atlantis, Cape Town (2024)

Treated at our Atlantis clinic.

Watch her story on YouTube
"Before, I used to burn my hand, things fell out of my hand… after the Bowen therapy, while she was working on me, my hand — and three fingers — started to warm up. It is now two weeks since the first session. Improvement. I can do things with my hand which I could not do before — I can make my hand straight, my hand feels warm before it was always cold, now I can use my fingers because there is more feeling."
Daniel Arries — Atlantis, Cape Town (2024)

Karrima Nortje's client. Treated for stab wounds to the left arm.

Watch his story on YouTube
"I am Tim Smith, I am 78. Fifty-five years ago I had a very serious car crash — it messed up my neck, messed up three lumbar discs, shattered my right femur, and messed my knee up. And I have lived in considerable pain for the last 55 years. And thanks to Bowen therapy, I am on the right track to healing."
Tim Smith — Muizenberg, Cape Town (2025)
