Become Involved

Add your hands to this work.

Two ways in: internships for work experience, and volunteering for people who can give their time. Both are small commitments that show up in the communities we serve.

Internships

Work experience in a real community health project. A small stipend for food and travel is available.

Community Clinic Coordinator (Intern)

Help our trainees run their community clinics: booking clients, preparing the space, keeping the records straight, and making sure every visitor is looked after.
